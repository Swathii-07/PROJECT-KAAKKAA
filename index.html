🐦 THE CONCEPT

Can we turn a crow into a crane?

Our project was based on the Malayalam chollu “കാക്ക കുളിച്ചാൽ കൊക്കാവില്ല”.

We imagined what would happen if someone genuinely tried to transform a crow into a crane.

That became our game.

  • 🧼 Bathe the crow
  • 🫧 Soap it
  • 🧴 Shampoo it
  • 💇 Comb it
  • 🎨 Paint it white
  • 🦵 Make it balance on one leg
  • 📏 Make its legs longer

And after all of that... it is still a crow.

🛠 BUILD MODE

Making the useless project work

We worked on turning the idea into an interactive game where the player could actually participate in Kakka's transformation.

We worked with animations, interaction, sound and different stages of the crow's transformation.

We also experimented with webcam-based hand interaction so that the player could interact with the game using their hand.
